        <h1 style="text-align: center;">📍 On-site 📍</h1>
        <br>
        <div class="info-grid">
            <div class="info-card">
                <h2>💶 Money</h2>
                <p>The local currency in France is the Euro (€).</p>
                <p>Cash is not always necessary but useful for small purchases, taxis, or markets.</p>
                <p>ATMs are widely available in Nice and Cannes.</p>
            </div>
            <div class="info-card">
                <h2>🔌 Power Outlets</h2>
                <p>France uses Type C and Type E power outlets with a standard voltage of 230V.</p>
                <p>You will need a plug adapter (and possibly a converter) if traveling from the US.</p>
            </div>
        </div>
        <br>
        <br>
        <div class="info-grid">
            <div class="info-card">
                <h2>🗣️ Language</h2>
                <p>The official language is French.</p>
                <p>In Nice and Cannes, many people speak English in hotels, restaurants, and shops.</p>
                <p>A few basic French words are always appreciated!</p>
            </div>
            <div class="info-card">
                <h2>💳 Credit Cards</h2>
                <p>Visa, Mastercard, and American Express are widely accepted.</p>
                <p>Contactless payments (Apple Pay, Google Pay) are very common.</p>
                <p>Still, we recommend carrying a small amount of cash.</p>
            </div>
        </div>
        <br>
        <br>
        <div class="info-grid">
            <div class="info-card">
                <h2>🚗 Transport</h2>
                <p>Uber and Bolt are widely available in Nice and Cannes.</p>
                <p>Public transportation includes a simple bus network across Cannes.</p>
                <p>Taxis are available but can be more expensive.</p>
                <p>If you're staying in central Cannes, many places are walkable.</p>
            </div>
            <div class="info-card">
                <h2>☀️ Weather</h2>
                <p>The French Riviera typically enjoys warm and sunny weather.</p>
                <p>Expect mild to warm temperatures, with cooler evenings.</p>
                <p>We recommend light clothing, sunglasses, and a light jacket for the evening.</p>
            </div>
        </div>
